Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 June 2026

This policy is provided as a starting point and should be reviewed by a qualified legal adviser before the site is made public to confirm it reflects Clinical Property’s actual practices and current Australian privacy law.

Clinical Property (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, hold and disclose your personal information, and how we comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information we collect

We may collect personal information that you provide to us directly, including:

  • your name, company and role;
  • contact details such as email address and phone number;
  • the contents of enquiries, correspondence and meeting notes; and
  • information relevant to a property advisory engagement you ask us to undertake.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit this site (such as your browser type and pages viewed) to help us maintain and improve it.

2. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiries and provide our advisory services;
  • communicate with you about engagements, opportunities and updates;
  • maintain our business records; and
  • comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Disclosure and data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose it to trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business (for example, IT, email and professional advisers), and where required or authorised by law. Where information is disclosed to third parties, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with this policy.

Third-party services. We use the following services that may collect or process your personal information:

  • Calendly (online consultation booking): collects the details you provide when scheduling, such as your name, email address and chosen time. See calendly.com/privacy.
  • Attio (customer relationship management): stores your contact details and our communications with you so we can manage our relationship. See attio.com/legal/privacy.

These providers may store data outside Queensland, and we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle it appropriately.

Rx Property Australia. By providing your information to us, you consent to us sharing it with Rx Property Australia, where applicable, for interstate dealings and as a partnering agency. This allows us to assist you with matters outside South East Queensland and to coordinate on engagements where Rx Property Australia acts as a partner. Rx Property Australia handles any shared information in accordance with its own privacy obligations.

4. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference and loss, and from unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies

This site uses minimal browser storage to operate (for example, to remember that you have entered the preview access password during your session). We do not use this information to identify you personally.

6. Access and correction

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete. To do so, contact us using the details below.

7. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us and we will respond as soon as practicable. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with the “last updated” date revised accordingly.
