# Clinical Property: Brand & Design System

> Source of truth: the official Clinical Property brand style guide supplied by
> the client. This document transcribes that guide for use by the website.

Clinical Property is a specialised medical real estate advisory, partnering with
investors, developers and occupiers to deliver premium healthcare property
outcomes across **South East Queensland**.

> **Geographic focus:** for practical and licensing reasons, Clinical Property
> operates with a dedicated focus on South East Queensland. This supersedes any
> "national / nationwide" wording in the original brand brief. Collateral must
> not claim coverage beyond South East Queensland.

**Brand line:** CLINICAL. STRATEGIC. TRUSTED.

---

## Primary logo

CP monogram (white "CP" on a teal→navy gradient rounded square) locked up with a
two-line wordmark: **CLINICAL** (deep navy) over **PROPERTY** (medium teal),
set in DM Sans. A reverse lockup (white wordmark) is used on navy backgrounds.

## Typography (prescriptive, Brand Identity Brief §03)

Two complementary sans-serifs: **DM Sans** for display/hierarchy, **Inter** for
body legibility. Both are free Google Fonts (SIL OFL), with no licensing required.

| Typeface | Role | Source |
|----------|------|--------|
| **DM Sans** | All display, headings and the `CLINICAL PROPERTY` wordmark | Google Fonts (free, OFL) |
| **Inter** | All body copy, tables, footnotes, captions | Google Fonts (free, OFL) |

### Type scale (use exactly)

| Level | Font / weight | Size | Leading | Usage |
|-------|---------------|------|---------|-------|
| Display | DM Sans **Bold 700** | 34-42pt | 1.15× | Cover / hero titles |
| Title | DM Sans **SemiBold 600** | 22-28pt | 1.20× | Page titles, section openers |
| Heading | DM Sans **SemiBold 600** | 14-18pt | 1.25× | Sub-sections, callouts |
| Body | Inter **Regular 400** | 10-11pt | 1.55× | All body copy |
| Caption / Label | Inter **Regular 400** | 8-9pt | 1.40× | Captions, footnotes, metadata |
| Tracked Caps | DM Sans **Bold 700 +80** | 8-9pt | 1.40× | Section labels, tags |

- The wordmark `CLINICAL PROPERTY` is DM Sans uppercase, tracked **+80**.
- If Inter is unavailable, DM Sans Regular 400 may substitute for body.

### ⛔ Do NOT use

**Montserrat, Poppins, or Lato**, overexposed in generic real estate and
wellness branding; they undermine the specialist positioning. (The earlier site
used Montserrat; that version is archived on the `archive/montserrat-version`
branch and must not be reintroduced.)

### Apply across ALL collateral

This typography system is mandatory for every Clinical Property asset: website,
investor CIMs, brochures, business cards, email signatures, LinkedIn/social
templates and proposals. Web pages load DM Sans + Inter via Google Fonts; for
print/Office, install the desktop families (free) and follow the scale above.

## Differentiation note (website)

The site deliberately avoids the "full-navy band with bright centred numbers and
vertical hairline dividers" stat treatment so it does not resemble the Rx Property
site. Metrics are instead presented on light, card-based layouts using the
teal→navy signature accent and the brand line-icon set.

The **signature teal→navy gradient** (the brand hallmark, `--cp-gradient`) is used
on the larger brand surfaces (the footer and the hero "At a glance" panel) rather
than flat navy. Flat navy is kept for the thin top utility bar.
